CredResolve · Platform architecture

One collections OS, four ways to reach a borrower

Every module feeds Collection OS, the system of record. Aastra decides the strategy, then three execution lanes carry it out: agentic digital channels, the tele-calling floor and field collections. Drona analyses every conversation and feeds what it learns back into the bots and the dialer.

Feeds into (data, actions, dispositions) Two-way insight loop between Drona and Naadh

Core

The single source of truth every lane writes back to.

Collection OS
Portfolios, accounts, allocations, dispositions, PTPs, payments and reporting across all channels.

Strategy & orchestration

Decides who to contact, on which channel, when and with what treatment.

Strategy engine AASTRA
Turns segments and workflows into contact strategies and pushes them to Collection OS.
Auto-segmentation AAYUDH
Groups borrowers by risk and propensity to pay. Feeds Aastra and Naadh directly.
Auto-workflow
Builds and runs treatment journeys without manual setup.
Control Tower
Operations view for monitoring and steering strategy performance.

Digital & agentic

AI-led, self-serve conversations at scale.

Agentic AI layer NAADH
Runs autonomous borrower conversations across every digital channel.
Agentic Voice
AI voice agents for reminders, negotiation and promise-to-pay capture.
Web Bot · WhatsApp Chat
Chat-based resolution and payment on web and WhatsApp.
Gen AI Video VISHWARUPA
Personalised video nudges generated per borrower.

Tele-calling

Human agents, augmented.

Dialer
Outbound calling for the agent floor, writing outcomes to Collection OS.
Agent Assist
Real-time prompts and guidance for agents on live calls.
Post-comm Analytics DRONA
Analyses calls and conversations after they end, and feeds learnings to the Dialer and Naadh.

Field collections

Doorstep visits, planned and supervised.

Field App JATAYU
The field agent's app for visits, outcomes and collections.
Lead Allocation & Prioritisation NAKHSA
Dynamically assigns and orders cases for each field agent.
Agent Assist · Voice Notes
In-app guidance and voice-captured visit notes.
Fleet Tower
Live supervision of the field fleet, reporting into Collection OS.
