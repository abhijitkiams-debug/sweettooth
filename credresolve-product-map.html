CredResolve · Platform architecture

One collections OS, four ways to resolve a loan

Aastra sets the strategy. Collection OS, the system of record, sends allocations to four execution lanes: agentic digital channels, tele-calling, field and legal. Every outcome comes back to it. Control Tower gives a 360° view of execution, and the whole stack runs on CredResolve's own LLM, speech-to-text and text-to-speech models.

Strategy & orchestration

Decides who to contact, on which channel, when and with what treatment.

Strategy engine AASTRA
Turns segments, workflows and execution insights into contact strategies and pushes them to Collection OS.
Auto-segmentation AAYUDH
Groups borrowers by risk and propensity to pay. Feeds Aastra and Naadh directly.
Auto-workflow
Builds and runs treatment journeys without manual setup.
Control Tower
360° execution insights and analysis across every lane, fed back into strategy.

Core

The single source of truth every lane reads from and writes back to.

Collection OS
Portfolios, accounts, allocations, dispositions, promises to pay, payments and reporting across all channels.

Digital & agentic

AI-led, self-serve conversations at scale.

Agentic AI layer NAADH
Runs autonomous borrower conversations across every digital channel.
Agentic Voice
AI voice agents for reminders, negotiation and promise-to-pay capture.
Web Bot · WhatsApp Chat
Chat-based resolution and payment on web and WhatsApp.
Gen AI Video VISHWARUPA
Personalised video nudges generated per borrower.

Tele-calling

Human agents, augmented.

Dialer
Outbound calling for the agent floor, writing outcomes to Collection OS.
Agent Assist
Real-time prompts and guidance for agents on live calls.
Post-comm Analytics DRONA
Analyses calls and conversations after they end, and feeds learnings to the Dialer and Naadh.

Field

Doorstep visits, planned and supervised.

Field App JATAYU
The field agent's app for visits, outcomes and collections.
Dynamic Lead Allocation & Prioritisation NAKHSA
Assigns and orders cases for each field agent.
Agent Assist · Voice Notes
In-app guidance and voice-captured visit notes.
Fleet Tower
Live supervision of the field fleet, reporting into Collection OS.

Legal VAJRA

Legal recovery for accounts that need it.

Notice Management
Generates, sends and tracks legal notices.
Litigation Management
Tracks cases, hearings and outcomes through the legal process.
Win Rate Predictor
Estimates the likelihood of a favourable outcome before a case is filed.

STT
